RESPECTFULLY SHEWETH:

1. The petitioners are bona fide agricultural title-holders and agriculturalists of Village Padra whose private agricultural lands measuring 28.45 hectares under Survey Nos. 301 to 345 have been notified for compulsory acquisition under Section 11 of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

2. GROSS VALUATION DISPARITY BETWEEN CIRCLE RATE AND MARKET TRANSACTION RATES:
   (a) The competent authority has proposed base compensation by adopting the outdated Jantri / Circle Rate of Rs. 420 per square meter (fixed in year 2011).
   (b) The actual prevailing fair market transaction value in the immediate vicinity of the industrial corridor, as evidenced by certified registered sale deeds (Document Nos. 1422/2023 and 1895/2023 registered at the Sub-Registrar Office, Padra), ranges between Rs. 1,450 to Rs. 1,680 per square meter.
   (c) The prevailing market value is more than 3.5 times higher than the circle rate adopted by the revenue department, violating Section 26(1)(a) of the Act.

3. MANDATORY CRITERIA UNDER SECTION 26 OF THE ACT OF 2013:
   Under Section 26(1), the Collector is legally bound to assess market value based on the higher of:
   - The average sale price indicated in registered sale deeds for similar lands in the vicinity during the preceding three years (taking the highest 50% of the sale deeds).
   - The circle / guideline rate.
   Arbitrarily relying on the 2011 circle rate while ignoring 2022-2023 registered commercial sales constitutes a blatant statutory violation.

4. STATUTORY REMEDY & PRAYER:
   The petitioners hereby pray that the Special Land Acquisition Officer:
   (i) Reject the proposed circle rate of Rs. 420/sq.m for award calculation under Section 23.
   (ii) Convene the statutory Direct Negotiation Committee under Section 26 Proviso 4, headed by the District Collector, to determine mutual consensus market compensation with the affected landowners.
   (iii) Include 100% Solatium under Section 30 and rural multiplier factor of 2.0 as per the First Schedule.

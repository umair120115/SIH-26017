REPORT OF THE MULTI-DISCIPLINARY EXPERT GROUP
SOCIAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT (SIA) EVALUATION
UNDER SECTION 7 & SECTION 41 OF THE LARR ACT, 2013

PROJECT: East-West Dedicated Freight Corridor (Jharkhand Mineral Corridor)
LOCATION: District Ranchi & Khunti (Fifth Schedule Tribal Area)
DATE: 20th January, 2024

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXPERT COMMITTEE FINDINGS ON TRIBAL LAND SAFEGUARDS & GRAM SABHA RESOLUTIONS
UNDER SECTION 41 AND SECTION 42 OF RFCTLARR ACT, 2013
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW:
The Multi-Disciplinary Expert Group constituted under Section 7 of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013 has evaluated the Social Impact Assessment (SIA) report for the acquisition of 320 hectares of land in the Scheduled Areas of Khunti District.

2. COMPLIANCE WITH SECTION 41 (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR SC/ST LAND):
(a) The proposed alignment intersects 45% tribal land owned by members of the Munda and Oraon Scheduled Tribes.
(b) Section 41(1) of the Act strictly states that acquisition of land in Scheduled Areas shall be an absolute exception.
(c) Section 41(3) requires prior mandatory consent of the concerned Gram Sabhas or the Autonomous District Councils at all stages of acquisition and SIA.
(d) 14 Gram Sabhas were convened in local Mundari and Sadri dialects between October and December 2023. 12 Gram Sabhas passed formal resolutions supporting the infrastructure alignment subject to comprehensive tribal rehabilitation.

3. MANDATORY STATUTORY ENTITLEMENTS MANDATED BY THE EXPERT GROUP:
In accordance with Section 41 and the Second Schedule of the Act:
(i) One-third (33%) of the total compensation amount shall be paid to affected tribal families upfront as first installment before any land acquisition award is declared.
(ii) A dedicated Tribal Development Plan (TDP) must be prepared and sanctioned by the Commissioner of Rehabilitation & Resettlement, ensuring 100% resettlement in alternative contiguous tribal habitats.
(iii) Fishing rights, minor forest produce collection rights, and community forest title under FRA 2006 must be legally restored in the relocated resettlement colony.

4. FINAL RECOMMENDATION:
The Expert Group recommends clearance of the SIA subject to strict execution of the Tribal Development Plan under Section 41(4) prior to publication of the Section 19 Declaration.

INTERIM ORDER & JUDICIAL STAY
UNDER SECTION 10 OF THE LARR ACT, 2013
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Heard learned Senior Counsel appearing for the writ petitioners and learned Additional Advocate General for the State respondents alongside Counsel for the National Highway Authority of India (NHAI).

2. The subject matter of challenge in this writ petition is the Section 11 Preliminary Notification dated 12.08.2023, issued for the acquisition of 142.50 hectares of prime agricultural land in the District of Purba Bardhaman for the construction of the Economic Freight Express Corridor.

3. Learned Counsel for the petitioners has demonstrated through certified Revenue Records (Khatian / RoR) that over 74.2% of the subject land comprises multi-crop irrigated agricultural land possessing assured perennial canal irrigation infrastructure developed under State Command Area schemes.

4. STATUTORY MANDATE UNDER SECTION 10 OF THE LARR ACT, 2013:
   Section 10(1) and Section 10(2) of the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 impose a statutory bar and food security ceiling:
   "No multi-crop irrigated land shall be acquired under this Act except under exceptional circumstances, as a demonstrable last resort..."
   Furthermore, Section 10(4) mandates that whenever multi-crop irrigated land is acquired, an equivalent area of cultivable wasteland must be developed for agricultural purposes, or an amount equal to the investment cost deposited with the State.

5. A perusal of the Social Impact Assessment (SIA) report approved by the Expert Group reveals that no feasibility survey was conducted to explore contiguous barren or single-crop waste lands prior to notifying the petitioners' triple-cropped fertile paddy land.

6. PRIMA FACIE CASE AND BALANCE OF CONVENIENCE:
   This Court is satisfied that the respondent authorities have acted in clear breach of the mandatory food security safeguards enshrined under Section 10(1) and Section 10(2) of the Act of 2013. Irreparable injury would be caused to the agrarian community if the land is forcefully dispossessed.

7. OPERATIVE ORDER:
   (i) There shall be an immediate INTERIM STAY on all further acquisition proceedings, including the issuance of any Section 19 Declaration and Section 23 Award, in respect of the multi-crop irrigated plots covered under Khasra Nos. 102 to 245 of Mouza Shaktigarh.
   (ii) The District Land Acquisition Officer (DLAO) is directed to constitute a joint inspection committee with the Directorate of Agriculture to verify the irrigation status of the acquired parcels and submit a comprehensive report within six (6) weeks.
   (iii) List this matter for further hearing on 28th April, 2024.

(JUSTICE S. K. BANERJEE)
